## Weekly Rotation

### Monday — Text-Only Observation
**Format:** 200-400 characters, one insight from your week.
**Example angle:** "I sat in on a sales call this morning where the rep spent 8 minutes explaining features the prospect never asked about..."
**Why it works:** Raw, immediate, feels like you're talking to one person.

### Tuesday — Carousel / Document Post
**Format:** 3-4 slides made in Canva, one idea per slide.
**Example angle:** "3 signs your sales process has a leak" — slide 1: leads sit for days, slide 2: reps do CRM admin instead of selling, slide 3: no one owns the handoff.
**Why it works:** High engagement, saves, shares. LinkedIn algorithm loves documents.

### Wednesday — Video (when HeyGen quota resets)
**Format:** 60 seconds, you talking to camera. Or HeyGen avatar if you're not camera-ready.
**Example angle:** "The one automation that saved me 4 hours a week at Vodafone."
**Why it works:** Video gets 5x more engagement than text on LinkedIn. But only if it's genuine — no scripted corporate speak.

### Thursday — Voice / Audio
**Format:** ElevenLabs voice post or native LinkedIn voice message.
**Example angle:** 90 seconds on "The qualification question no one asks but everyone should."
**Why it works:** Audio feels personal. People listen while commuting.

### Friday — Newsletter Promo
**Format:** Snippet from this week's tip + link.
**Example angle:** "This week's tip is about the handoff that kills 30% of qualified leads. Here's the first paragraph... [link to full]"
**Why it works:** Drives newsletter signups from your LinkedIn audience.

### Saturday — Light / Personal
**Format:** Text, low stakes.
**Example angle:** Rock music reference, something you read, a thought about Dublin tech scene.
**Why it works:** Shows you're a person, not a brand account.

### Sunday — Off or Reshares Only
**Why it works:** Rest. Or reshare something valuable someone else posted with your take.
