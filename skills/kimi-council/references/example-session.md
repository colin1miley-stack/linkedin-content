# Kimi Council — Example Session

## Input

> User: "Council review: Should we launch in 6 weeks or 12?"

## Advisor Responses

**Strategist:** "12 weeks. A rushed launch with broken trust is a tax you pay for 18 months. Sequencing beats speed."

**Skeptic:** "Neither timeline accounts for supplier MOQ risk. We're assuming they'll say yes."

**Creative:** "What if we launched a 'founding member' beta in 6 weeks? Scarcity beats polish."

**Operator:** "6 weeks means no Airtable CRM, no Klaviyo flows, no 3PL locked. We'd be hand-packing orders."

**Audience Advocate:** "I don't care about your timeline. I care that my first sachet arrives intact and tastes okay."

## Peer Review Highlights

- Response C (Creative) strongest: beta idea breaks the false binary
- Response A (Strategist) blind spot: ignores the cost of delaying revenue
- All missed: the "founding 50" beta could be a standalone revenue stream, not just a test

## Chairman's Verdict

**Where the council agrees:** Speed matters, but not at the cost of broken trust. The question isn't 6 vs 12 — it's what can we validate fastest.

**Where the council clashes:** Strategist + Operator want delay; Creative wants beta now; Skeptic distrusts the premise entirely.

**Blind spots caught:** The beta could be a revenue stream, not just validation.

**Recommendation:** Launch a limited "Founding 50" beta in 8 weeks. Run it manually with a simple Typeform → Stripe → Airtable pipeline. Validate taste, packaging, and messaging before full launch.

**One thing to do first:** Confirm supplier sample pricing and MOQ this week.

## Next Steps

- (This week) Confirm supplier sample pricing and MOQ (Colin)
- (Week 2) Build beta landing page + Stripe checkout
- (Week 4) Recruit 50 founding members via organic + warm outreach
- (Week 6) Ship beta sachets, collect feedback
- (Week 8) Go/no-go for full launch based on NPS + reorder intent

**Key Risk:** Supplier fails to deliver samples on time → have backup white-label option pre-qualified.
