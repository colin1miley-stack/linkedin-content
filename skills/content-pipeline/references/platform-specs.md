# Platform-Specific Rules

| Platform | Max Length | Tone | Media |
|----------|-----------|------|-------|
| LinkedIn | 1,300 chars | Professional but human | 1 image or video |
| Newsletter | 2,000 words | Deep dive, structured | Header image |
| YouTube Shorts | 30-60 sec script | Punchy, visual | HeyGen video |
| Twitter/X | 280 chars | Sharp, opinionated | Optional |
