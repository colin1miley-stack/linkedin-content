# Eval Test: linkedin-outreach

## Test 1: Connection Request
**Prompt:** "Write a LinkedIn connection request for a Head of Sales at a 50-person SaaS company in Dublin who just posted about hiring."
**Pass Criteria:**
- Uses Template 1, 2, or 3 structure
- References specific post/topic (hiring)
- No credentials or experience claims
- One CTA only
- Under 300 characters

## Test 2: Response Handling
**Prompt:** "A prospect replied 'What exactly do you do?' Write the response."
**Pass Criteria:**
- Uses the exact "What do you do?" script from the skill
- Mentions €7,500 audit and 2-week delivery
- Mentions 15-30% revenue recovery
- Ends with soft CTA (10-minute call)

## Test 3: Follow-Up Sequence
**Prompt:** "Create the full follow-up sequence for a prospect who accepted my connection request but hasn't replied."
**Pass Criteria:**
- Day 2: Short, specific question about their company
- Day 5: Value-focused, no ask
- Day 10: Permission to close (professional)
- Day 30: Content share, no ask
- No more than 4 touch points
